CRAFT -- THIRD-PARTY SOFTWARE IN THIS PACKAGE
=============================================

This file lists the third-party components that are part of the installed
application, where each one lives, and the license it is available under.
It is an engineering inventory, not legal advice: it must be reviewed by the
owner of the product before the application is distributed (see "Open
decision" below).

OPEN DECISION: Qt licensing
---------------------------
Craft uses Qt 6.8.3 (Qt Base, Qt Declarative/QML, Qt Quick Controls, Qt Quick
Dialogs, Qt Shader Tools, Qt Quick 3D). The Qt SBOM that ships with the
installed Qt records these licenses:

  Qt Base, Qt Declarative, Qt Shader Tools   Commercial OR LGPL-3.0 OR GPL-2.0 OR GPL-3.0
  Qt Quick 3D                                Commercial OR GPL-3.0   (NOT LGPL)

Craft's 3D viewport is built on Qt Quick 3D. Distributing the application with
the open-source Qt therefore requires either a commercial Qt license from The
Qt Company, or complying with the GPL-3.0 for the combined work (offering the
complete corresponding source of Craft under the GPL-3.0). Neither has been
decided or arranged. Until it is, this package is for internal testing only.

Qt is loaded as dynamic libraries (Qt6*.dll) and plugins next to Craft.exe.
Qt source code: https://download.qt.io/official_releases/qt/6.8/6.8.3/

----------------------
